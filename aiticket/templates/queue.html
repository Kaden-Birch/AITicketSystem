How the queue works

Waiting

Temporary failures retry automatically. Maintenance or ticket silence can delay delivery.

Needs review

Failed and expired messages need review. Check the destination before requesting a retry.

Skipped

Messages no longer needed, or excluded by notification policies, are skipped. An opening alert may be skipped after recovery.

The queue survives application restarts. Retrying a notification does not restart an AI investigation.
