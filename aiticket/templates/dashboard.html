About health indicators

Host health follows the Hosts page. Critical check failures count as needing attention; an intentional shutdown is shown separately. Enabled checks with missing or stale results are unknown, not healthy. Disabled and manual checks are excluded. Checks in maintenance are shown separately; existing failures may still be recorded. “Verified down” means the check reached its configured failure threshold, not that every service on the machine has failed.

About resolution and human intervention

Resolution measures elapsed time from creation to closure, including waiting and manually confirmed resolutions. Human intervention includes a recorded blocker, human work session or takeover; creating a manual ticket alone does not count. The backlog includes tickets opened before this period. Days with no closures have no average resolution value. Historical backlog follows recorded creation and closure times.
