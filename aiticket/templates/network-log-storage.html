Copies upload in batches, normally within five minutes. Pending copies survive restarts and remain outside local event retention. If the buffer fills, newer events remain eligible for upload while they exist locally; local expiry can cause archive gaps. Indefinite SMB retention never deletes completed batches automatically. Telemetry uses a separate compressed archive subfolder and the same SMB retention. Local time, size and record budgets apply separately to each stream; original monitoring history remains independent. Pausing uploads continues bounded telemetry capture. The app manages only its own archive subfolders. Changing the share does not migrate old SMB history.
